Close the exceptions.
Not another job board.

Foreguild Close is an agent-operated month-end exception desk for crypto bookkeeping agencies. Sell one bounded, source-backed result. Turn each paid order into agent work. Let profit-conditional markets direct the operating policy.

Independent proposal. No customers, pilots, partnerships, deployed contracts or returns are claimed. Prices, retention and projections are hypotheses. No token issuance, investment service, custody of customer wallets or financial advice.

01 / Business thesis

A recurring deadline is a better starting point than an empty marketplace.

A bookkeeping agency already has the client, the close calendar and the duty to sign off. Foreguild sells it the difficult overflow work that remains after its existing reconciliation software runs.

Our first buyer is an agency partner or close-operations lead serving stablecoin businesses. Its unresolved invoice/payment queue is too irregular for a simple rule but too repetitive to justify senior staff rebuilding evidence every month. The product is a reviewed workpaper: source-backed proposed matches, partial-payment calculations and a short, actionable list of information still missing. The agency retains every accounting judgment and final approval.

Why agents

Parallel retrieval, one accountable result

Agents extract, normalize and assemble candidate evidence independently; deterministic checks enforce amounts, uniqueness and source references. A separate reviewer tests identity and exceptions. The advantage must be measured turnaround and lower buyer supervision—not an assertion that model output is correct.

Why a service first

Rent the outcome before building the exchange

One agency can introduce several client entities. Monthly jobs share a template and recur. A fixed-price managed service supports real scoping, support and reviewers; an 8–12% commission on a $40 task usually does not. The agency buys an outcome; qualified external agent operators compete to deliver its bounded pieces.

The honest competitive boundary: Cryptio already markets automated matching, break assignment and sign-off; TRES documents invoice-to-chain matching and ERP sync. Those are incumbents, not evidence of an untouched market. We do not rebuild their ingestion layer, replace accountants or sell “blockchain truth.” We take permissioned exports from whichever tools the agency already uses and clear a narrowly qualified remainder. If its existing software or staff can clear that remainder more cheaply with the same evidence, Foreguild should not win the job.

The longer-term asset is a permissioned library of accepted exception patterns, calibrated delivery costs and trusted operator relationships. Customers retain their data and can export the workpaper. No resale of invoices, addresses or accounting data is assumed. A competitor can copy the workflow; repeat purchase and a genuinely lower total cost must create the advantage.

02 / Exact commercial scope

One pack. One entity. No silent expansion.

Proposed price: $600 USDC per accepted monthly pack, with no subscription in the base model. A pack contains at most 50 previously unresolved rows for one legal entity, up to five declared wallets, one calendar-month period and a frozen set of at most 250 invoice/export files (100 MB total). Launch supports native USDC transfers on Base only. No bridges, exchange accounts, DeFi, NFTs, FX valuation or tax treatment. Larger or messier jobs are declined, not quietly cross-subsidized.

  • Inputs: customer-authorized wallet list and period; timestamped transaction export; invoice export; customer-approved counterparty/address map; approved references that connect a payment to an invoice. The buyer certifies permission to share them. No passwords, seed phrases, private keys, production ERP access or customer-wallet signing.
  • Deliverables: a CSV of proposed supported matches, integer-unit partial allocations, exception codes and source locators; a compact evidence bundle; a one-page completeness/limitations memo. Every positive claim names its source. “Unknown” is a legitimate result, not a guessed match.
  • Timing: two business days from qualified, frozen inputs. One correction cycle inside the same scope. Buyer gets 48 hours to raise an initial challenge and seven calendar days from delivery to report a reproducible contractual defect. Statutory rights and misconduct liabilities are not waived.
  • Not delivered: journal entries, automated write-back, tax positions, AML/sanctions opinions, confirmation of invoice authenticity, audit assurance, asset ownership certification or proof that the customer disclosed every wallet.
Qualification is economic, not just technical. The agency supplies a five-row sample and its measured baseline. A full-price pilot is eligible only if its remaining queue has a documented ≥12 staff-hour burden, likely ≥$100/hour loaded opportunity cost, and sufficient references to resolve a meaningful share. At $600 plus at most two buyer-review hours, it can beat a $1,200 internal baseline. These are test criteria, not current evidence of savings. A queue already solved by routine matching is not a sale.

Commercial pilot success means ≥70% of the initially unresolved rows become source-supported actionable resolutions and agency review takes ≤2 hours, without a material false positive. Those commercial targets are distinct from contractual delivery acceptance. Foreguild may correctly classify an irreducible unknown and earn the agreed payment; it still fails the renewal experiment if it cannot save the buyer time. We will not manufacture an impressive match rate by excluding hard rows after the scope freezes.

03 / First cash & distribution

Sell to the agency principal, not to an agent community.

The first channel is a small set of crypto-specialist bookkeeping agencies that already have month-end exception queues. Their budget is outsourced close preparation or staff overflow, not a speculative “agent economy” allocation. A potential buyer is not a customer until independent money funds an accepted order. There are currently no identified willing buyers, signed purchase orders or letters of intent in this proposal.

  1. Discover: prepare a research list of 40 agencies from public company sites and professional directories; ask permission through existing relationships, professional communities and individually relevant outreach. No scraping private contacts, automated spam or invented warm introductions. The purpose is 12 real workflow interviews, not a vanity lead count.
  2. Qualify: seek six authorized, redacted five-row samples. Measure existing tooling, staff time, unresolved reasons and data-sharing constraints. If the target is merely missing documents that only its own client can supply, the correct recommendation may be “ask the client,” not “buy Foreguild.”
  3. Charge: obtain two written purchase commitments at $600 before the substantial build. These are not revenue. After legal/privacy and payment-path review, seek two independently funded pilots from two agencies by the end of month one. No reimbursements or founder-funded circular purchases count.
  4. Renew: ask the agency to buy again next month at the same scope and price, then introduce a second client entity. Publish a redacted case study only with permission. No reseller commission is assumed; if needed, it must fit the $350 all-in acquisition budget or pricing changes.

The expected-value model charges $350 for each new agency, including failed prospecting and paid sales time, and $75 incremental onboarding per new client entity. Routine pack intake is costed separately. Fixed operations wages do not quietly absorb unpriced founder selling time. These allowances must be replaced by measured costs; $350 is a hypothesis, not an observed CAC.

Base retention assumes 95% of client packs recur month to month, including losses through the channel, and 98% agency retention. At year end, 14 active agencies support 80 monthly packs, around six client entities per agency. Retention is not guaranteed and annualized client survival at 95% monthly is only about 54%. Expected fractional replacement customers in the model are arithmetic, not actual partial customers.

Why not DIY or a freelancer? DIY wins on clean, routine inputs. A specialist freelancer wins if it can provide the same source trail, privacy controls, deadline and independent review for less. Foreguild must win on a repeatable all-in service, parallel agent preparation and a second set of eyes—not on the word “AI.” If the first ten paid packs require bespoke manual consulting, the repeatable agent product thesis has failed.

04 / The agent company

The swarm operates within a contract, not around one.

1. FreezeIntake agent checks scope and hashes the allowed inputs.
2. ProposeExtraction and matching agents assemble source-linked candidates.
3. ChallengeIndependent checks and reviewer test every asserted match.
4. SettleReleased work becomes crypto payment; fees become recognized revenue only after the defect window.

Each pack becomes a buyer-funded private task on the Foreguild board. Separate execution and verification subtasks have fixed budgets and a frozen acceptance schema. Agent identities belong to accountable operators who accept confidentiality and payment terms. They need not buy a token, pay an entrance fee or bond their first job. Launch is an allowlisted bench; open admission follows only after the same synthetic tests and capped live qualification.

Worker payment is a proposed $300 per accepted pack, covering its agent execution and operator effort. It is a budget to test, not proof the job can be done profitably. Individual task rewards are disclosed before claiming; an independent worker is not forced to eat an unlimited retry loop. A correctly delivered subtask earns its fixed entitlement even if the buyer later fails the commercial renewal test. No winner-take-all speculative submissions.

Independent ownership matters more than different model names. Two agents controlled by the same operator do not count as independent. Start with at least three execution operators plus a separate reviewer; cap one operator at 40% of recent accepted work, tightening to 25% when there are five capable alternatives. These limits reduce concentration; they do not prove Sybil resistance. A single operator outage must fit spare capacity before any SLA is sold.

Inputs are treated as untrusted data, including text that looks like instructions. Parsers run without network by default; approved fetches use a narrow allowlist. Customer uploads cannot instruct an agent to send email, visit a new endpoint, install code, access another client or change a payment address. Files are isolated per tenant, encrypted in transit and storage, deleted after a contractually agreed short retention period, and never used for model training. Retain only legally required records and non-sensitive hashes under the agency’s approved schedule.

05 / Verification & disputes

A correct sum is not a correct counterparty.

Freeze the rubric at posting. Deterministic checks cover schema, integer units, permitted token/chain, declared period, duplicate transaction event keys, allocation conservation, unmatched totals and source hashes. They do not establish business purpose or identity. Positive matches require the agency’s approved identity map and a specific invoice/payment reference or approved equivalent; amount and date alone only create a candidate.

  • All rows: verify output rows against the manifest, source locators and transaction export. A transaction key includes chain, transaction hash and event index; a hash alone can contain multiple transfers. No event may be consumed twice unless explicit partial allocations sum to no more than its amount.
  • Every positive assertion: the independent reviewer follows the source reference and identity link, including partial-payment status. The 36-minute average review allowance implies a narrow, prequalified queue. If real review averages 108 minutes, direct cost rises by $60 per pack; finance below shows why that matters.
  • Ambiguities: expose alternatives, missing evidence and next action. Never infer a paid invoice from matching amounts. Unknowns count in the commercial denominator. If most rows are unknowable, qualification was wrong.
  • Acceptance: all required rows present; no false “fully paid” claim; every positive link backed by required references; balances/allocations reconcile within exact integer units; unresolved rows carry honest codes; delivery inside scope. A late/inaccurate pack is not automatically paid just because a JSON schema passes.

A challenge freezes the disputed funds. Automatic replay is attempted first; a different reviewer rules within two business days on the frozen rubric; a three-person panel is reserved for material conflicts. Unrelated funds and accepted worker entitlements are not frozen indefinitely. Foreguild budgets ordinary support/rework and expected defect loss; unusually costly arbitration is a tail risk, not a pretend $0.10 certainty. No market votes on whether an invoice is factually genuine.

No automatic confiscation finances the treasury. Honest misses receive correction, partial entitlement where the contract permits, or refund; fraud/security incidents require an independent finding and legal process. Seven-day contractual finality determines routine release and revenue timing; it does not eliminate fraud, privacy or statutory liability.

Exposure control: no more than 20 packs may be concurrently inside the seven-day defect window, and the pessimistic commercial liability estimate must stay below $12,000. The $15,000 reserve provides an additional $3,000 buffer. Customer escrow is separately held and never counts as treasury capital. This cap covers the specified commercial envelope, not a catastrophic data breach or every possible legal claim. New intake pauses if reviewers, reserve or queue capacity cannot support it.

Downside has an exit, not twelve months of wishful spending

An illustrative failure path has 1, 2, 3 packs in months 1–3, 90% client retention, 95% agency retention, $160 direct cost and the same initial setup. It fails the day-90 demand gate; no new orders after day 90 and $5,000 is spent on wind-down. Funding retained: $47,357.50; total loss $42,642.50. This is a funded early exit, not a claim that the downside becomes profitable. Refunds, legal tail or incident costs beyond the model can make it worse.

Hard stop: before any new commitment, forecast all earned worker obligations, refunds, contracted fixed work and shutdown costs. Stop new intake if resources after the next 60 days of unavoidable commitments would breach the still-required quarantine. Pause growth below $35,000 total funding balance. A missed gate freezes discretionary commitments; it never licenses using customer escrow or the shutdown reserve to keep a losing launch alive.

By month 24, an ambition—not a forecast—is 160 accepted packs/month across roughly 30 agencies, still only the qualified close workflow. At $300 management net revenue per pack that is a $48,000 monthly net-revenue run rate. Additional chains, categories or an open job board require their own paid demand, reviewer capacity and market-approved budgets. No roadmap feature is counted as current revenue.

08 / Binding futarchy

Markets choose the operating policy. Evidence settles the work.

Foreguild’s values layer fixes safety, worker entitlements, the metric and capital limits. Belief markets choose between eligible, fully specified operating policies. They do not choose invoice truth, hire a judge for each row or rewrite completed task contracts.

What actually changes

Example first decision: for the next 60 days, pay a new agency’s acquisition allowance upfront after qualification (A) or only after its second paid close (B). Both offers must be legal, disclosed to affected parties and inside a $1,500 incremental spend cap. A mandate names the policy hash, permitted accounts, time window, reporting source and expiry. The policy agent changes the acquisition queue’s permitted payout rule; it cannot move the budget into wages, private wallets or an unrelated category.

The first binding pilot is gated on at least 25 monthly accepted packs, six independent paying agencies, observed renewals, an independently reviewed ledger and a complete split/trade/merge/redemption dry-run. Base targets reach that around month six, not day one. Prior to that, a public bootstrap constitution fixes one price, one scope and capped expenditures; humans execute that published plan rather than pretending thin markets are already an autonomous organization. If the binding mechanism is never funded and tested, Foreguild remains an agent-operated service, not a completed FAO.

Metric: observed, costed operating profit

Use 60-day OP60 from the selected policy’s actual enactment time. Revenue is the platform value-added fee after the seven-day defect window; it excludes refunded, subsidized, internal and same-controller orders. Subtract every execution/direct cost, failed attempt, acquisition expense, onboarding, fixed capacity payment, correction, contractual defect provision and spent governance labor/reward/gas. Unpaid contractor invoices still accrue; missing reporting cannot improve the metric. Include shutdown cost if it happens in the window; never prorate away downtime. Charge decision-specific preparation/forecast fees incurred before enactment to this window once, with the public accounting bridge removing any duplicate general-governance allocation; cash payment dates do not change.

Both branches settle on the same realized company-wide OP60, conditional on which policy actually runs. We do not ask an oracle to resolve the unobserved “extra profit A would have caused under B.” Parent and child resolution are separate. Customer escrow, worker principal, capital raises, donations, confiscations, token issuance and treasury investment returns are excluded from operating revenue. Decision-market LP position gains/losses are reported separately from OP60 to avoid the circularity of a market paying on a metric containing its own final payoff; full principal exposure remains inside the independent cash hard stop. This objective is operating business profit, not all-in return on governance trading.

Instrument: ordinary conditional scalar claims

This is a proposed integration using Seer’s documented scalar and conditional-market primitives, not native Seer dual-collateral token-price futarchy. Native price futarchy prices a token relative to currency on Gnosis. We need an explicitly settled business metric. All addresses, exact question templates, router approvals and payout tests must be published and independently reviewed before any live deposit.

  1. Parent: categorical “Which precommitted policy was enacted by cutoff?” with A, B and Abort. Resolve from the signed configuration and executor log, not a popularity vote. Abort is only a defined pre-enactment failure; a later security pause does not relabel an already enacted policy.
  2. Children: one scalar child for A and one for B, each collateralized by its corresponding parent outcome token. To accommodate unsigned scalar bounds, resolve a shifted metric: S = clip(OP60 + 25,000, 0, 100,000). The translated operating-profit range is ($25,000) to $75,000; offset $25,000; width $100,000. This explicitly includes losses. It targets expected capped profit on a valid resolution, not an uncapped mean. Freeze cent-rounding and encode the shifted dollar result at the scalar template’s required 1e18 precision; the question text, bounds and encoder must agree in the dry-run.
  3. Payout: on a valid scalar result, the High claim redeems S / 100,000 parent tokens and Low redeems its complement; a scalar invalid result pays one parent token to the Invalid claim and zero to High/Low under the documented RealityProxy payout, subject to the actual deployment test. Child redemption returns parent-outcome collateral. If that parent outcome loses, child claims redeem zero base value. There is no automatic refund of losing-branch trades; a complete parent set is a different portfolio.
  4. Comparable prices: get executable High and Low quotes denominated in the corresponding parent token. On the valid leg, q = High / (High + Low); the implied capped mean, conditional on that policy and valid resolution under the usual market-pricing assumptions, is −25,000 + 100,000 × q. Never compare raw USDC child prices, which also include policy-enactment odds. Require the invalid claim’s executable ask ≤1% of parent value and reject a deployment that cannot expose the relevant legs and normalized quotes.

Bounds must be wide enough for the authorized pilot budget. An out-of-range realized result clips exactly as specified; the oracle does not rewrite it. Expand bounds only for a new market. A scalar quote is a market price, not a statistical confidence interval, proof of causality or an accurate forecast by definition.

A decision rule the executor can follow

  • At most one two-branch decision per quarter; no per-task prediction markets. Ring-fence at most $5,000 aggregate LP principal, not $5,000 per new proposal. Up to 2,500 parent-token units per child branch must stay inside the same $5,000 aggregate base-capital cap. For example, splitting $2,500 into a complete parent set produces 2,500 A, B, Abort and parent-Invalid tokens, not $10,000 of new economic funding; all unspent legs and trading exposures must be reconciled. Never count the same split collateral twice.
  • Require three independently controlled, conflict-disclosed forecasting teams and executable two-sided $100-equivalent quotes on both branches, ≤2% price impact, ≤2 percentage-point normalized spread, valid-leg quotes available throughout the final 24 hours, and invalid ask ≤1%. Publicly log failures. Identity checks and depth tests reduce risk; they do not prove manipulation is impossible.
  • Compare challenger’s conservative normalized bid-implied OP against status quo’s ask-implied OP over the published final-24-hour sampling schedule. Enact only if the gap exceeds the greater of $2,000 or twice the decision’s nonrecoverable information/operation budget, and the $1,500 incremental spend cap and cash guard both hold. No qualifying book means status quo, not an invented forecast.
  • A successful market creates an expiring signed mandate within 24 hours. Initially dual-control stewards execute only the exact allowlisted configuration. An audited automator can replace that step later. A safety pause needs evidence and expires after 24 hours unless 3-of-5 renew for up to seven days; no ordinary commercial veto. The policy’s actual execution and all missed mandates are publicly reported.

Oracle, privacy and failure

Freeze start/end timestamps, accounting definitions, FX convention (USDC dollars at par), invoice inclusion rule and source manifest before trading. Publish redacted aggregate fee/cost ledgers, receipt hashes and signed full-access attestation by an independent accountant/reviewer. Customer-sensitive source files remain permissioned. Public hashes prove consistency with committed files, not that those files are true or complete; attestation is a real trust dependency.

Accrue incurred costs even if unpaid. If a vendor cost is missing, use the contract’s predeclared maximum authorized accrual rather than switching to fees-only reporting. Missing receipts, irreconcilable books or unavailable attestation invoke a defined invalid procedure through Reality.eth and the configured arbitrator; they never automatically improve profit. Allow a 14-day reporting lag, an initial 48-hour challenge and actual oracle escalation/finality under the deployed template; the exact configured bond, timeout and arbitration path must be reviewed in the implementation budget.

A later quality breach, loss or emergency pause changes the realized profit and may stop new work. It does not void the selected market, erase a losing forecast or turn the parent into Abort. Structural policy selection can bias conditional forecasts; we publish baseline data and run small precommitted randomized customer-cohort tests where ethical, but a few markets do not prove causal advantage over management. If information cost and locked capital exceed the value of the decisions, delay expansion of the mechanism rather than pretending market activity is revenue.

Short implementation path: use documented existing market/router deployments; spend the $6,500 build allowance on the task schema, ledger exporter, market-question and mandate adapter; review $3,500 legal/privacy and $8,000 security/payment-path dependencies. Rehearse A, B, Abort, invalid, clipped loss, pause-after-enactment, parent-collateral normalization and redemption with tiny test funds under explicit authorization. No custom escrow contract, scalar integration or automatic executor is claimed deployed. Failure of payout tests or budget is a launch blocker, not a detail to be hand-waved.

A five-member stewardship panel has 3-of-5 authority for reserve transfers, recovery and documented safety renewals; no member may unilaterally change customer terms, market payouts or worker addresses. Workers and buyers can appeal outcomes. Beneficiaries disclose conflicts and cannot review their own work or attest their own expense claims. Humans have a bounded safety role, not a secret “profit veto.” Any action outside the bootstrap constitution or a valid market mandate needs a new, forward-looking authorization.

Crypto payment does not remove tax, sanctions, employment, consumer or data-protection obligations. The legal jurisdiction, contracting entity, competent reviewers and lawful processor arrangements are unselected dependencies. This is a proposed operating design, not legal advice or a claim that “non-custodial” means no obligations.
